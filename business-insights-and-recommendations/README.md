# 💡 Data-Driven Business Insights Report

This document presents key business insights derived from the Snowflake SQL analysis conducted for the E-commerce Business Performance & Profitability Intelligence project.

The analysis focuses on annual business performance, sales growth, channel performance, product profitability, discount effectiveness, customer demographics, and customer segmentation. Each insight includes key findings, a business recommendation, and the potential business impact.

---

## 🔍 Detailed Analytical Findings

### Q9. Annual Gross Sales, Net Sales, Total Cost and Profit
- *Key Business Insights:*
  - Annual net sales remained relatively stable, ranging from approximately *12.64M to 13.36M* between 2016 and 2025.
  - 2018 recorded the highest net sales at *13.36M, while 2017 recorded the lowest at **12.64M*.
  - 2023 generated the highest annual profit at *5.32M*, representing the strongest profit result during the reported period.
  - In 2025, net sales declined to *13.00M* and profit declined to *5.19M*.
- *Business Recommendation:* Investigate the sales and cost drivers behind the stronger performance in 2023 and the decline in 2025. Use these findings to improve sales planning and cost control.
- *Business Impact:* Identifying the drivers of stronger and weaker years can help maintain stable revenue and protect profitability.

### Q10. Annual Profit Margin Percentage
- *Key Business Insights:*
  - Annual profit margins remained consistently close to *40%* throughout 2016–2025.
  - The highest margin was approximately *39.91% in 2025, closely followed by approximately **39.91% in 2023*.
  - The lowest margin was approximately *39.48% in 2021*.
  - The small variation suggests relatively stable profitability as a percentage of net sales.
- *Business Recommendation:* Maintain the existing level of profitability while reviewing the pricing, discounting, and cost factors that contributed to margin fluctuations.
- *Business Impact:* Consistent profit margins support predictable financial performance and help the business make more reliable pricing and budgeting decisions.

### Q11. Year-over-Year Sales and Profit Growth
- *Key Business Insights:*
  - Both net sales and profit declined in 2017 by approximately *4.52% and 4.84%*, respectively.
  - In 2018, both recovered, with net sales growing by *5.72%* and profit by *5.55%*.
  - The strongest annual profit growth in the reported period occurred in 2018. The year 2023 also showed positive growth in both sales and profit.
  - In 2025, net sales declined by *2.23%* and profit by *1.52%* compared with 2024.
  - Sales and profit growth generally moved in the same direction, indicating that changes in sales were accompanied by similar changes in profit.
- *Business Recommendation:* Investigate the causes of the declines in 2017 and 2025, and identify the business drivers behind the recovery in 2018 and the growth in 2023.
- *Business Impact:* Monitoring year-over-year trends helps identify performance risks early and supports timely business decisions.

### Q14. Sales and Profitability Across Sales Channels
- *Key Business Insights:*
  - The *Website* was the leading sales channel, generating net sales of *62.47M* and profit of *24.78M*.
  - The *Mobile App* ranked second, with net sales of *42.37M* and profit of *16.81M*.
  - The *Marketplace* generated net sales of *26.38M* and profit of *10.49M*.
  - All three channels had similar profit margins, ranging from approximately *39.66% to 39.78%*.
  - Marketplace had the highest margin at approximately *39.78%*, despite having the lowest total sales.
- *Business Recommendation:* Maintain the Website as a major sales channel while exploring opportunities to increase Mobile App and Marketplace sales. Investigate whether Marketplace growth can be achieved without reducing its margin.
- *Business Impact:* Improving sales across channels while maintaining their existing margins can increase overall revenue and profit.

### Q16. Top 10 Products by Net Sales
- *Key Business Insights:*
  - *Books Product 277* ranked first, generating net sales of approximately *1.59M*.
  - *Computing Product 341* ranked second at approximately *972K, followed by **Storage Product 238* at approximately *888K*.
  - *Fragrance Product 672* and *Learning Product 121* also performed strongly, generating approximately *877K and 839K* in net sales, respectively.
  - The top 10 products include products from multiple categories, indicating that sales performance is not limited to a single category.
- *Business Recommendation:* Prioritize stock availability and sales monitoring for leading products, particularly Books Product 277. Review the sales drivers of other top-performing products to identify opportunities for growth.
- *Business Impact:* Better inventory planning for high-sales products can help reduce missed sales opportunities and support revenue growth.

### Q21. Product Quantity Sold and Average Selling Price Across Categories
- *Key Business Insights:*
  - *Fashion* recorded the highest quantity sold at *56,072 units, with an average selling price of **415.51*.
  - *Electronics* ranked second in quantity sold at *51,279 units* and had an average selling price of *459.50*.
  - *Beauty & Personal Care* recorded the lowest quantity among the six categories at *40,219 units, but the highest average selling price at **470.82*.
  - *Sports & Outdoors* had the lowest average selling price at *397.58, despite selling **50,523 units*.
- *Business Recommendation:* Maintain inventory availability in Fashion, which leads in quantity sold. Investigate whether targeted promotions or improved product visibility can increase Beauty & Personal Care sales volume. Evaluate pricing and product mix in Sports & Outdoors.
- *Business Impact:* Category-specific inventory and pricing strategies can help improve sales volume while protecting revenue per unit.

### Q22. Relationship Between Discount Rates and Profit Margins
- *Key Business Insights:*
  - Products sold without discounts generated net sales of approximately *32.42M* and a profit margin of *46.02%*.
  - The *0–10% discount range* generated the highest net sales, at approximately *54.31M, with a profit margin of **41.44%*.
  - The *10–20% discount range* had a lower margin of *34.81%*.
  - The *20–30% discount range* had the lowest reported margin at *26.34%*.
  - The results show a clear pattern: higher discount ranges are associated with lower profit margins in this dataset.
- *Business Recommendation:* Review discount effectiveness and prioritize lower discounts where they can generate sufficient sales. Before offering deeper discounts, assess whether the additional sales volume compensates for the reduced margin.
- *Business Impact:* More disciplined discounting can protect profit margins while still using promotions to generate sales.

### Q28. Net Sales and Profit Across Customer Age Groups
- *Key Business Insights:*
  - Customers aged *55 and above* contributed the highest net sales of *55.67M* and profit of *22.07M*.
  - The *44–54 age group* ranked second, generating net sales of *21.15M* and profit of *8.40M*.
  - The *25–34 and 34–44 age groups* generated similar net sales, at approximately *20.78M and 20.67M*, respectively.
  - Customers below 25 contributed the lowest net sales at *12.95M* and profit of *5.17M*.
- *Business Recommendation:* Prioritize customer retention and relevant product offers for the 55-and-above segment. Explore targeted campaigns to increase engagement and spending among younger customers.
- *Business Impact:* Age-group-based marketing can help retain valuable customers while identifying opportunities to grow underperforming segments.

### Q29. Unique Customers, Orders, Net Sales and Profit by Gender
- *Key Business Insights:*
  - *Male customers* generated net sales of *63.35M* and profit of *25.17M* across 7,181 customers and 36,242 orders.
  - *Female customers* generated net sales of *61.37M* and profit of *24.37M* across 6,967 customers and 35,004 orders.
  - Male customers contributed slightly higher total sales and profit than Female customers.
  - The "Prefer not to say" group generated net sales of approximately *5.28M, while the Non-binary group generated approximately **1.23M*.
  - Male and Female customers represented the largest customer groups and sales contributions in the reported results.
- *Business Recommendation:* Continue serving the largest customer groups effectively, while using inclusive, behavior-based marketing to understand and engage other customer groups. Validate differences in average order value and purchase frequency before making segment-specific decisions.
- *Business Impact:* A customer-focused and inclusive marketing strategy can help improve engagement and support informed decisions about customer retention and growth opportunities.

### Q31. Most Profitable Customer Segments by Age and Gender
- *Key Business Insights:*
  - *Regular Male* customers generated the highest net sales (*25.99M) and profit (10.31M*).
  - *Regular Female* customers followed closely, generating net sales of *25.67M* and profit of *10.20M*.
  - *Budget Male and Budget Female* customers generated approximately *7.08M and 7.06M* in profit, respectively.
  - *Premium* customers generated lower total profit than Regular and Budget customers in both the Male and Female groups.
  - *VIP* customers generated comparatively lower total profit, suggesting a smaller contribution to overall profit in this dataset.
- *Business Recommendation:* Focus on retaining Regular customers through loyalty initiatives and personalized offers. Analyze purchasing behavior among Premium and VIP customers to identify opportunities to increase their contribution without assuming that segment size alone indicates customer value.
- *Business Impact:* Customer-segment strategies can support retention, targeted marketing, and improved profitability across customer groups.

### Q39. Top Three Most Profitable Products Within Each Category
- *Key Business Insights:*
  - *Beauty & Personal Care:* Fragrance Product 672 led with approximately *411.33K profit*, followed by Fragrance Product 250 and Haircare Product 618.
  - *Books & Stationery:* Books Product 277 led with approximately *640.10K profit*, followed by Learning Products 121 and 639.
  - *Electronics:* Computing Product 341 was the most profitable, generating approximately *242.52K profit*.
  - *Fashion:* Watches Product 085 led with approximately *269.18K profit*.
  - *Home & Kitchen:* Storage Product 238 ranked first, generating approximately *265.10K profit*.
  - *Sports & Outdoors:* Cycling Product 592 led with approximately *237.33K profit*.
- *Business Recommendation:* Monitor inventory and profitability for these category-leading products. Evaluate their pricing, cost structure, and sales consistency to understand what drives their performance. Consider promoting profitable alternatives within each category.
- *Business Impact:* Focusing on profitable products can improve product-mix decisions, support category-level planning, and help allocate inventory more effectively.

- *Overall Business Recommendations*
Based on the selected findings, the following actions are recommended:
1.	*Monitor annual performance:* Investigate significant changes in sales and profit to improve planning and cost control.
2.	*Protect profit margins:* Review discount policies and pricing decisions to reduce unnecessary margin erosion.
3.	*Optimize sales channels:* Build on the Website's strong contribution while evaluating opportunities across the Mobile App and Marketplace.
4.	*Improve inventory planning:* Monitor leading products by sales and profitability to support product availability and better inventory allocation.
5.	*Apply category-specific strategies:* Evaluate category-level sales volume, average selling price, and profitability before changing pricing or promotional strategies.
6.	*Strengthen customer retention:* Develop relevant engagement and loyalty initiatives using observed purchasing behavior rather than assumptions based solely on demographics.

- *Conclusion*
-The analysis highlights relatively stable annual profit margins, differences in sales channel contribution, variation in product performance, an association between higher discount levels and lower profit margins, and differences in customer-segment contributions.
These findings can support more informed decisions about sales planning, pricing, promotions, product selection, inventory management, and customer engagement.
